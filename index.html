How it works

Two models run one after the other on your server. The first repairs the print, the second adds color.

  1. 1

    Upload the damaged photo

    Scratches, creases, dust, fading and small tears are all fine. Scan or photograph it flat, in even light.

  2. 2

    Add a color reference

    Pick a color photo with a similar subject and light: a portrait for a portrait, a garden for a garden. Its palette guides the result.

  3. 3

    Restore and colorize

    The server repairs the damage, then transfers color from your reference. It can take a minute or so. Download either version.
